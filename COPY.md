## 08 One platform
**Everything they see, in one place.**
Air and ground data flow into a single view of every site, and our AI surfaces what needs attention before it costs you.
- **Progress:** track work against plan, quantify earthworks and see what's really done.
- **Quality:** catch defects early: cracks, corrosion, leaks and installation errors, ranked by severity.
- **Safety:** spot hazards automatically and keep people out of harm's way.

## 09 How it works
1. **Capture:** drones and robots collect data on a schedule you set.
2. **Understand:** AI processes it into 3D models, comparisons and flagged issues.
3. **Act:** your team gets clear reports and alerts, with no hardware to manage.
