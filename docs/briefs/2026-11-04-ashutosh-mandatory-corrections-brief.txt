1. NON-NEGOTIABLE: DO NOT TURN THIS INTO A CINEMATIC INTRO FOLLOWED BY A CONVENTIONAL WEBSITE
The website must remain one continuous cinematic experience from the Northstar logo animation to the final contact screen.
Not wanted: logo then drone sequence, a few robot sequences, cinematic ending after the product introductions, then conventional Technology, Solutions, Industries, About Us and Contact sections. Also not wanted: a video simply placed behind conventional sections.
The cinematic is the website itself. Products, information, applications, platform demonstration, brand story and contact information must all be presented within it.

Required architecture: one persistent full-screen cinematic stage throughout.
1. Logo animation transitions into the cinematic.
2. Drone enters and introduces its capabilities.
3. Drone exits naturally, leading into the humanoid sequence.
4. Humanoid introduces its capabilities and applications.
5. Humanoid transitions naturally into the quadruped sequence.
6. Quadruped introduces its capabilities and applications.
7. Physical robotics environment transitions into the Northstar Platform.
8. Platform appears as an interactive foreground interface within the cinematic.
9. Vision chapter communicates the complete ecosystem.
10. Final Contact chapter concludes the journey.
Solutions, Industries, technical specifications, product information, About Us and other existing content must be incorporated into these scenes. No separate conventional sections after the product introduction.

How information must be integrated: contextual foreground elements over the cinematic. Use connecting lines from the actual products, product identification labels, compact information panels, expandable technical-information overlays, contextual application descriptions, minimal HUD-inspired indicators, and interactive elements where required. When more information is needed, add more reveal states in the same journey, not static pages or sections. Example: the drone has multiple reveal moments covering its complete product information; same for humanoid and quadruped. Platform stays interactive inside the stage. Vision and Contact stay part of the same experience.

Not acceptable: ending the cinematic after the quadruped with a conventional site below; separate full-screen static sections for Technology, Solutions, Industries or About Us; conventional image-and-text layouts; standalone product cards or feature grids; a fixed cinematic hero followed by ordinary sections; each product as a separate video section with an unrelated background; Platform, Vision or Contact as disconnected pages.
Use a persistent stage with a scroll-controlled narrative timeline and foreground layers. Keep native scrolling responsive and natural. The cinematic must remain present and relevant throughout the page.

2. DEVICE MOVEMENT AND CINEMATIC CONTINUITY (fix at source-footage and animation level)
A. Drone: consistent body orientation relative to direction of flight; gradual turns; yaw and banking before turns; camera follows actual movement; no abrupt changes in screen position or perspective; realistic flight speed. Never travelling one way while facing another without a visible turn.
B. Humanoid: consistent walking direction and body orientation. When changing direction: feet pivot, torso follows, head and body settle into the new direction, camera synchronized. Do not join clips with inconsistent walking directions.
C. Quadruped: consistent orientation; complete turning movements; leg movement matches direction of travel; no sudden forward-facing to backward-facing changes; consistent camera tracking.
D. Natural exits: all three products currently exit unnaturally, often accelerating or sprinting out of frame. Required for every product: (1) completes movement or reveal, (2) resumes at natural speed, (3) gradually approaches destination, (4) decelerates or adjusts direction, (5) camera follows, (6) moves behind a physical object, through an entrance or into the distance, (7) camera continues, (8) next environment becomes visible naturally. Do not animate the product out of the viewport. Do not accelerate playback to force an exit. Do not abandon the camera move before the departure completes.

3. DRONE PROPELLERS: CONTINUOUS MECHANICAL MOVEMENT
Propellers currently stop during flight and start again. During hover and reveal states: position stable, all propellers rotating, rotor motion and blur consistent, never looks powered off while airborne. Do not freeze the video on a still frame while hovering. Separate continuous mechanical movement from scroll-controlled movement: a continuously playing hover sequence or an independently controlled propeller animation or composited layer. Apply the same principle to subtle idle movement in humanoid and quadruped where appropriate.

4. INDUSTRIAL ENTRANCE AND SHUTTER
The exterior establishes an overhead horizontally slatted roller shutter opening vertically. The next shot introduces a different door mechanism with structures closing from left and right, which then open during the camera move. Required: retain the overhead roller shutter; it opens vertically; must not close from left and right; sufficiently open before the camera approaches; stays open as the camera passes; consistent entrance dimensions, walls and architecture; remove the left-right door animation; no other open/close animation during the zoom. Reduce excessive smoke and fog around the entrance so building and doorway are clear. The architecture must not change as the camera travels through.

5. SCROLL PERFORMANCE: FIX THE UNDERLYING BUGS (critical)
Scrolling is laggy, occasionally stops responding, and does not consistently synchronize. Do not fix by reducing sensitivity, adding delays or making the visitor scroll faster.
Investigate: competing ScrollTrigger instances; duplicate scroll controllers; repeated video currentTime updates; seeking during raw scroll events; excessive simultaneous decoding; large inefficient video files; multiple pinned sections with conflicting spacers; excessive React state updates; heavy backdrop filters and compositing; conflicting smooth-scroll libraries; incorrect ScrollTrigger refresh logic; unnecessary DOM calculations.
Required: one authoritative scroll controller; persistent stage; GSAP ScrollTrigger controls the timeline; animation and video-seeking coordinated through requestAnimationFrame; only the active scene seeks where possible; preload the immediate next scene; do not load all videos at once; no reloads when moving backwards; no competing pin and snap; native scroll responsiveness. Video, information animations and platform interactions must not block scroll input. Profile the actual problems and verify improvements with browser performance tools.

6. INFORMATION PRESERVATION: ABSOLUTELY NO CONTENT REDUCTION
The objective was to change presentation, not reduce information. The current build has lost substantial product, technical, solution and industry information. Reducing, deleting, shortening or omitting approved information is not approved.
First inspect the original approved content and create a complete inventory. Preserve:
- Drone: full descriptions, technical specifications, features, capabilities, workflows, applications.
- Humanoid: complete descriptions, technical details, features, capabilities, use cases, differentiators.
- Quadruped: complete descriptions, specifications, inspection capabilities, applications, use cases.
- Platform: all software features, modules, functionalities, AI capabilities, differentiators.
- Solutions: all categories, descriptions and supporting information.
- Industries: all categories and their applications.
- About Northstar: company information, mission, vision, approved brand messaging.
- Contact: contact details, enquiry information, legal information, form functionality.
Distribute across multiple stopping points. Example drone journey: (1) introduction and primary purpose, (2) technical capabilities, (3) core features, (4) inspection workflows, (5) applications and use cases, (6) additional specifications. Humanoid and quadruped follow the same approach with their actual approved information. Panels appear progressively; expandable overlays for longer explanations. The Platform can hold a more extensive interface. Visitors must be able to reach all original approved information without a conventional section.
Content-mapping document required, columns: Original approved content / Current implementation / Missing information / Reduced or modified information / Cinematic chapter / Exact panel or overlay. Every item accounted for. Final implementation retains 100% of approved content, presentation changes only. Do not invent capabilities or specifications.
