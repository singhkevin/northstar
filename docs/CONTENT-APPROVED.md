06. FEATURED SOLUTION — CONSTRUCTION
INTELLIGENCE


Every Site. Every Stage. Verified.
AI-powered construction quality assurance and progress intelligence.
Construction environments are dynamic, complex, and constantly changing. Traditional
inspections are periodic, heavily manual, and often fragmented across teams and systems.

Northstar combines aerial reality capture, ground robotics, computer vision, and agentic AI to
support more consistent, evidence-driven construction monitoring and quality assurance.

Our goal is to provide contractors, developers, engineers, and project owners with a clearer
understanding of what has been built, how work is progressing, and where intervention may
be required.

Construction Progress Intelligence

Monitor site changes over time using aerial and ground-level observations. Compare
captured progress with project schedules, work packages, and planned construction
activities.

Build-versus-Design Verification

Use reality capture and spatial analysis to compare observed construction conditions with
BIM models, approved drawings, and design specifications, identifying potential
discrepancies for engineering review.

Automated QA/QC Inspections

Support inspection checklists, workmanship observations, installation verification, defect
identification, and evidence-based quality reporting throughout the construction lifecycle.

Safety Observation & Risk Intelligence

Identify visible safety concerns and potential site hazards through AI-assisted observations,
helping safety teams prioritize investigations and corrective measures.

Quantity & Work-in-Place Verification

Support earthwork volume measurements, installation progress assessment, and verification
of work-in-place quantities where capture accuracy and measurement methods meet project
requirements.

Digital As-Built Documentation

Build a chronological, spatially referenced record of site conditions, supporting handover
documentation, issue resolution, project coordination, and future asset management.

Inspection Evidence & Traceability

Maintain georeferenced, time-stamped observations with associated images, inspection
findings, responsible parties, verification status, and supporting documentation.
See what is happening. Understand what has changed. Verify what has been
delivered.
