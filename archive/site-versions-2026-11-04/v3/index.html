AI-powered construction quality assurance and progress intelligence.

Construction environments are dynamic, complex, and constantly changing. Traditional inspections are periodic, heavily manual, and often fragmented across teams and systems.

Northstar combines aerial reality capture, ground robotics, computer vision, and agentic AI to support more consistent, evidence-driven construction monitoring and quality assurance.

Our goal is to provide contractors, developers, engineers, and project owners with a clearer understanding of what has been built, how work is progressing, and where intervention may be required.

  1. 01

    Construction progress intelligence

    Monitor site changes over time using aerial and ground-level observations. Compare captured progress with project schedules, work packages, and planned construction activities.

  2. 02

    Build-versus-design verification

    Use reality capture and spatial analysis to compare observed construction conditions with BIM models, approved drawings, and design specifications, identifying potential discrepancies for engineering review.

  3. 03

    Automated QA/QC inspections

    Support inspection checklists, workmanship observations, installation verification, defect identification, and evidence-based quality reporting throughout the construction lifecycle.

  4. 04

    Safety observation & risk intelligence

    Identify visible safety concerns and potential site hazards through AI-assisted observations, helping safety teams prioritize investigations and corrective measures.

  5. 05

    Quantity & work-in-place verification

    Support earthwork volume measurements, installation progress assessment, and verification of work-in-place quantities where capture accuracy and measurement methods meet project requirements.

  6. 06

    Digital as-built documentation

    Build a chronological, spatially referenced record of site conditions, supporting handover documentation, issue resolution, project coordination, and future asset management.

  7. 07

    Inspection evidence & traceability

    Maintain georeferenced, time-stamped observations with associated images, inspection findings, responsible parties, verification status, and supporting documentation.
